# Supervivo Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

Supervivo is an iOS app made by Devsworker ("we", "us"). This policy covers what personal data is involved when you use the app or contact us, why, and what rights you have. It applies wherever you use the app, including the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland.

## 1. The short version

- What you enter in the app (spending, income, cards, loans, goals, settings) stays **on your iPhone**. We never receive it and can't see it.
- There's no account to create, and the app has no ads, analytics or tracking.
- We only get personal data from you if you write to us, either by email ("Send feedback" in the app) or through the contact form on our website.

## 3. What we handle, why, and the legal basis

| Data | Why | Where it's kept | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Your records in the app: amounts and dates of spending and income, notes, categories, card names and payment days, loans, goals, fixed expenses, settings | To work out your daily budget and show it in the app and widgets | On your device only | None needed: we never receive this data |
| Feedback emails (optional): your email address, your message, and the app and iOS version that the app adds to the email | To answer you and fix bugs | Our mailbox (section 6) | Art. 6(1)(b), to handle your request; Art. 6(1)(f), our legitimate interest in fixing bugs |
| Contact form (optional): your email address, your name if you give it, your message and the page language | To answer you | Our mailbox (section 6) | Art. 6(1)(b), to handle your request |

We don't handle card numbers, bank account numbers, ID numbers, special categories of data (Art. 9 GDPR), your location or your contacts. We don't profile you or make automated decisions about you.

You can use every feature of the app without ever writing to us.

## 4. Device permissions

Every permission is optional. If you say no, only the feature that needs it stops working.

| Permission | What it's for | How it works |
|---|---|---|
| Notifications | Reminders for payday, the end of the day and card goals | Scheduled on your iPhone; no server is involved |
| Camera and Photos | Reading a receipt so you don't have to type it in | The photo is read on your iPhone with Apple's built-in text recognition and is never uploaded. If you keep the photo with a record, it stays on your iPhone |

Face ID lock uses the iOS "Require Face ID" setting. The app never sees your face data. Widgets are built from the records on your iPhone and send nothing anywhere. You can hide amounts on the Lock Screen in the app's settings.

## 5. Internet connections

The app downloads a few public files, such as exchange rates and public holiday lists, from GitHub (GitHub, Inc., USA). These requests carry none of your records and no identifier. Like any website, GitHub may see technical data such as your IP address. We don't receive it. GitHub's handling of that data is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

## 6. Who else is involved, and transfers outside the EEA/UK

We don't sell or share your personal data.

- **Contact form:** your message goes through Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA), which runs the form, and Resend (Resend, Inc., USA), which delivers it to our mailbox as an email. Neither keeps the message for us. Your IP address is checked briefly to stop spam and isn't stored.
- **Our mailbox:** emails are received and stored with Apple's iCloud Mail (Apple Inc., USA; for EEA users, Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland). They may be stored in the USA or other countries where Apple runs data centers.

For transfers to the USA, we rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (including its UK and Swiss extensions) where the provider is certified, and on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses otherwise.

We are based in the Republic of Korea. The European Commission has decided that Korea offers an adequate level of data protection (adequacy decision of 17 December 2021).

## 7. How long we keep it

- **Your records in the app:** they're gone when you delete them in the app or delete the app. We have no copy.
- **Emails and contact form messages:** kept for one year after your question is answered or the issue is fixed, then deleted, including from the trash. We keep them longer only if the law requires it.

## 8. Backups

Backup files (.supervivo) and CSV exports contain your records and aren't encrypted. Where you keep them and who you share them with is up to you.

If iCloud Backup is turned on for your iPhone, your records may be included in it. That backup belongs to your Apple Account and is covered by Apple's privacy policy. We can't access it.

## 9. Your rights

For anything you've sent us by email or through the contact form, you can ask us to:

- show you what we have and give you a copy (Art. 15), also in a portable format (Art. 20);
- correct it (Art. 16) or delete it (Art. 17);
- limit how we use it (Art. 18);
- stop using it where we rely on legitimate interests (Art. 21).

Just email help@devsworker.com. We'll reply within one month. Your records in the app you can edit or delete yourself at any time.

You can also complain to a data protection authority, in particular the one in the EU country where you live or work. In the UK, that's the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

## 10. Cookies and tracking

The app uses no cookies, no advertising identifier (IDFA) and no analytics or tracking tools. It doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

## 11. Children

The app doesn't collect personal data or ask for your age. If you're under 16 (or a lower age that applies in your country), please ask a parent or guardian before writing to us. If we find out a child has written to us without that permission, we'll delete the message.

## 12. Security

Your records sit in the app's private storage, protected by your passcode and iOS Data Protection. We don't run any server that collects personal data. Only we can access our mailbox, and it's protected by two-factor authentication.

## 13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and mention it in the app's update notes at least 7 days before it applies, or 30 days before for important changes to your rights.
