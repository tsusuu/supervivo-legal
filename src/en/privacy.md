
# Supervivo Privacy Policy

Effective date: [확인 필요: 1.2.0 출시일]

Devsworker ("we", "us") makes and runs the iOS app Supervivo ("the app"). This policy explains what personal data the app handles, why, and the rights you have. It applies wherever you use the app, including the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland.

## 1. Summary

- Everything you enter in the app (spending, income, cards, loans, goals, settings) is stored **only on your iPhone**. It is never sent to us, and we cannot see it.
- The app has no sign-up or login, and uses no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
- We receive personal data only if you choose to contact us, by email ("Send feedback" in the app) or with the contact form on our website.

## 3. What we process, why, and on what legal basis

| Data | Purpose | Where it is stored | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|---|---|
| App records: amounts and dates of spending and income, notes, categories, card names and payment days, loans, goals, fixed expenses, app settings | Calculating your daily budget and showing it in the app and widgets | Only on your device; never sent to us | We do not receive this data, so we do not process it as a controller |
| Feedback email (optional): your email address, the message, and the app version and iOS version added to the email | Answering you and fixing problems | Our mailbox (see section 6) | Art. 6(1)(b) (handling your request) and Art. 6(1)(f) (our legitimate interest in fixing errors) |
| Contact form on our website (optional): your email address, your name if you give it, the message, and the language of the page | Answering you | Our mailbox (see section 6) | Art. 6(1)(b) (handling your request) |

We do not process card numbers, bank account numbers, government ID numbers, special categories of data (Art. 9 GDPR), location or contacts. We do not make automated decisions or profile you.

You can use every feature of the app without ever sending us an email.

All permissions are optional. If you decline one, only the related feature stops working.

| Permission | Used for | How |
|---|---|---|
| Notifications | Payday, end-of-day and card goal reminders | Scheduled on your device; no outside server is involved |

Face ID lock uses the iOS "Require Face ID" feature. The app never accesses your face data. Widget amounts are made from the records on your device and are not sent anywhere; you can hide amounts on the Lock Screen in the app's settings.

## 5. Internet connections

The app downloads public files (such as exchange-rate and public-holiday lists) from GitHub (GitHub, Inc., USA). The app sends none of your records or identifiers in these requests. As with any internet connection, GitHub may process technical data such as your device's IP address; we do not receive or see it. GitHub's processing is covered by the GitHub Privacy Statement.

## 6. Recipients and transfers outside the EEA/UK

We do not sell or share your personal data. Messages sent with the contact form pass through Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA), which runs the form's server, and Resend (Resend, Inc., USA), which delivers them as an email to our mailbox. Neither stores the message for us. Your IP address is used for a moment to limit spam and is not stored. To receive and store feedback emails we use Apple's iCloud Mail (Apple Inc., USA, and Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland, for users in the EEA). Your email may therefore be stored in the USA or other countries where Apple has data centers.

For transfers to the USA we rely on the certification of Apple, Cloudflare and Resend under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions) [확인 필요: 게시 직전 Apple·Cloudflare·Resend DPF 인증 유지 여부 확인], and otherwise on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

We are based in the Republic of Korea, which the European Commission recognizes as providing an adequate level of data protection (Adequacy Decision of 17 December 2021).

## 7. How long we keep data

- **App records:** deleted from your device when you delete them in the app or delete the app. We hold no copy.
- **Feedback emails and contact form messages:** kept for one year after your question is answered or the problem is fixed, then deleted from the mailbox and trash, unless a law requires us to keep them longer.

## 8. Backup files and iCloud device backup

Backup files (.supervivo) and CSV files you export contain your records and are not encrypted. You decide where to keep them and who to share them with.

If iCloud Backup is on for your iPhone, your app records may be included in your device backup. That backup is handled by Apple under your Apple Account and Apple's privacy policy; we cannot access it.

## 9. Your rights

For data you sent us by email or the contact form, you have the right to:

- access it and get a copy (Art. 15), including in a portable format (Art. 20);
- correct it (Art. 16) or have it deleted (Art. 17);
- restrict its processing (Art. 18);
- object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21).

Email help@devsworker.com. We answer within one month. You can also edit or delete all app records yourself at any time in the app.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live or work. In the UK, this is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

## 10. Cookies and tracking

The app uses no cookies, no advertising identifier (IDFA) and no analytics or tracking tools, and does not track you across apps or websites.

## 11. Children

The app does not collect personal data and does not ask your age. If you are under 16 (or the lower age set by your country), please ask a parent or guardian before contacting us. If we learn we received a message from a child without that consent, we delete it.

## 12. Security

App records are stored in the app's private storage on iOS, protected by your device passcode and iOS Data Protection. We run no server that collects personal data. Our feedback mailbox is accessible only to us and protected with two-factor authentication.

## 13. Changes

If this policy changes, we will announce it on this page and in the app's update notes at least 7 days before it takes effect (30 days for important changes affecting your rights).
